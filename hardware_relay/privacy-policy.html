IKOT LA SALLE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Ikot La Salle passenger app collects, uses, and shares information when you use the app.

Information the app handles

  • Account and profile: passenger ID, name, user type, phone number, recovery email, and account/session details. Firebase Authentication manages sign-in credentials; the app does not store your password as plain text.
  • Ride and credit records: pickup and destination stops, vehicle, passenger count, fare, ride status, credits, top-up amount, GCash reference number, receipt image, and related review or notification records.
  • Location: your location while using map and ride features. While a passenger is onboard, the passenger app may periodically use that passenger device's GPS as the vehicle's live location. Vehicle hardware GPS is used when no passenger is onboard. Location is used for live maps, nearby stops, route progress, arrival checks, and safety/overstay functions.
  • Camera and photos: camera access is used to scan a vehicle QR code. If you submit a top-up, you choose a receipt image from your gallery; the app sends it for text recognition and stores it with your top-up request for review.

How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, recover passwords, provide live vehicle and route information, create and manage rides, calculate fares and credits, review top-up receipts, show ride and account notifications, prevent misuse, and provide support.

Services that process information

  • Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud database services for account, ride, location, top-up, and notification records.
  • Mapbox provides maps and map-related features. Map requests may include location or map-view information needed to display the map.
  • OCR.space receives a selected GCash receipt image to detect its amount and reference number. You can enter those details manually if scanning is unavailable.
  • Brevo delivers recovery-email verification and password-reset messages.

Authorized Ikot La Salle transport administrators can access information needed to manage accounts, rides, vehicle operations, and payment reviews. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Payments

Top-ups are requested by submitting payment details and a receipt for administrator review. The app does not process or store GCash account passwords or payment-card details. Any transfer to GCash is completed separately in the GCash service.

Storage, security, and retention

Information is stored in the app’s Firebase project and related service providers. We use access controls and secure network connections to protect it. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain account and operational records while they are needed to provide the service. You can request account and data deletion using the instructions below. Some transaction or security records may be retained when needed to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.

Request account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Ikot La Salle passenger account and associated personal data, email justinedaveocampo@gmail.com with the subject “Ikot La Salle account deletion.” Include your passenger ID so we can locate the account. Do not send your password.

We will verify the request and delete or de-identify account data that we are not required to retain. Any data kept for a legal, security, or transaction reason will be limited to that purpose.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for campus transport users and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact justinedaveocampo@gmail.com.
